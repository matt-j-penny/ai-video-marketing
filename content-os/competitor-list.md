`ig-competitor-research` and `yt-competitor-research` (each reads only its own platform
section) and by the two feed-research skills (anything from a listed account is always
a KEEP). Filled in by "apply my brand kit" from `brand-kit.md`; edit by hand any time.

**Order = priority.** Both competitor skills scan the top 5 handles in their section by
default. Name handles inline or say "all" to override.
