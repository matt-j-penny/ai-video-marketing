# Knowledge Index

This folder is the repo's compiled working memory: short, source-linked pages that summarize
what the research, transcripts, backbone, and voice corpus say, so ideation and scripting can
start here instead of re-reading 6 MB HTML reports. It is not a new source of truth.

**It starts empty.** After your first research runs (and after "apply my brand kit"), say
**"compile knowledge"** (the `knowledge-compile` skill) and it writes the topic pages listed
below from your own sources.

## Pages (created by knowledge-compile)

- `current-content-opportunities.md`: what the latest research says to make next.
- `winning-hooks.md`: reusable hook mechanics that keep showing up (hook-generator reads this).
- `competitor-patterns.md`: what competitors are doing that is actually working.
- `creator-ai-positioning.md`: your market lane, offer, and belief shifts (from backbone/).
- `reusable-script-formats.md`: proven structures for scripts and beat sheets.

## Source Rules

- `backbone/`, `voice-dna.md`, `voice-corpus/`, `research/`, and `transcripts/` stay authoritative.
- `knowledge/` is compiled memory. Useful, but disposable.
- Never invent proof, numbers, dates, or competitor stats here without a source path.
