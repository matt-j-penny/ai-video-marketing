# Per-video breakdown template

Each breakdown subagent returns this for every video it was given. Keep the whole report under ~900 words.

## <videoId>: "<title>" (<channel>, <views>, <outlier>x)

**Hook (0:00-0:30):** the opening lines near-verbatim, in quotes.
**Why it works:** one line. Payoff shown first? A pain point? A curiosity gap? The audience's own question? A before/after?

**Angle:** the core promise in one sentence.

**What the viewer came for:** what job this video does for the viewer, in a few words, plus the evidence. Don't force it into a fixed list. Examples of what it can be: a full start-to-finish tutorial, a quick setup, proof that a tool is good, a head-to-head verdict, a showcase of examples to steal, a money or career outcome, a reaction to news, a story, a teardown, a challenge or experiment, a resource list.

**Format:** the structure name, then the sections with approximate timestamps.

**Demos:** every concrete thing built or shown, one line each: what was made, how it was shown (timelapse, side-by-side, render), time and cost if given, what made it land and what failed. Specific enough that someone could recreate it.

**Tips:** every practical rule, gotcha or setup step the creator gives, one line each.

**Sponsor:** who, and how much of the video it shapes (or "none").
