# The 8 Hook Structures

The proven-hook bank (404 hooks, ranked by views) sorts into 8 spoken-hook
structures. This is the strategic layer: what each structure *does* to a viewer,
when to reach for it, and how to bend it to the creator's voice (`voice-dna.md`)
and the positioning in `backbone/messaging.md`. The live frameworks + view counts
come from `scripts/find_hooks.py --structure <name>`; this file is how you pick
which structure to pull.

Each structure below lists the **trigger** (why it stops the scroll), **best
for**, **your angle** (how to bend the shape to the creator's own positioning; the
one-line examples are illustrative only, from unrelated niches, not templates to
copy), and a representative real proof from the bank (the script output is the
ranked source of truth). Before writing, check `knowledge/winning-hooks.md` (when
it exists) for the creator's own hits in each shape; those outrank a bank row.

---

## 1. Educational/Tutorial — *146 hooks, the biggest bucket*

**Trigger:** promises a specific, usable skill or result fast. The viewer stays
because they think they'll walk away knowing how to do the thing.

**Best for:** "here's how I built / how this works" system demos, step-by-steps,
tool walkthroughs. Usually the workhorse bucket for anyone who teaches.

**Your angle:** lead with the *outcome* the creator's method produces, not the
mechanics; pull the outcome language from `backbone/messaging.md` and show the
thing itself in the first 3 seconds. Example (fitness creator): "If you swap your
last set for this one drill, your pull-ups double in a month."

**Proof:** *"He always trains his calves using full range of motion, and he only
does the top half. Who do you think will grow bigger calves?"* — 33.8M
([jeffnippard](https://www.instagram.com/jeffnippard/reel/C9FQ-W1OJtc/)).
Framework: `[X person] always [Y method], and [Z person] only [method]. Who grows bigger [result]?`

---

## 2. Secret Reveal — *117 hooks, the highest ceiling*

**Trigger:** dangles hidden knowledge / a thing "nobody tells you." Curiosity gap
the viewer has to close. This bucket holds the single biggest hit in the bank
(108M).

**Best for:** "the thing pros do that you don't," behind-the-curtain system
reveals, the unfair-advantage angle.

**Your angle:** position the creator's method as the secret the viewer's
competitors are quietly using; the secret must be something real the creator
actually does (proof bank in `backbone/messaging.md`), never a vague tease.
Example (real-estate agent): "Nobody fumbled a bag harder than sellers who still
list without staging."

**Proof:** *"Do you want to research a disease? ...Chances are you want to change
the world, but might not have the support."* — 108M
([goharsguide](https://www.instagram.com/goharsguide/reel/ClmgD_AJeBV/)).
Framework: `Do you want to [X]? [Y]? [Z]? Chances are you want [bigger goal], but lack [obstacle]. Introducing [solution].`

---

## 3. Contrarian/Negative — *48 hooks*

**Trigger:** attacks a sacred cow or names an enemy. Pattern-interrupt — the
viewer either agrees hard or rage-watches. Polarizing = shares.

**Best for:** hot takes, anti-guru positioning, "everyone's doing X wrong."

**Your angle:** name the belief or practice the creator's positioning is against
(the "enemy" in `backbone/messaging.md`) and match the heat to the register in
`voice-dna.md`; if the creator doesn't swear, the hook doesn't either. Keep it
punchy and earned, back the take immediately. Example (nutrition coach):
"Everyone's counting calories. Nobody's counting the meals they skipped."

**Proof:** *"Planes used to have vibe, now they don't. Cars used to... But good
design is about creating a feeling."* — 20.9M
([hansloreidesign](https://www.instagram.com/hansloreidesign/reel/C5rJryFoZZH/)).
Also the blunt *"Fuck your ChatGPT"* — 7M. Framework: `[X] used to [Y], now it doesn't. [repeat]. But [reframe].`

---

## 4. Raw Shock — *30 hooks*

**Trigger:** drops the viewer into a high-stakes / unbelievable moment mid-action.
No setup, pure "wait, what?" Energy and disbelief carry it.

**Best for:** jaw-drop demos, a result that sounds fake, in-the-moment reactions.

**Your angle:** a real number stated flat and fast, taken from the proof bank in
`backbone/messaging.md` (never invented or rounded up); show the receipt before
they can doubt it. Example (e-commerce founder): "It's nuts that most people will
never question this: I cut my ad spend in half and sales went up."

**Proof:** *"It's nuts that most people will never question this. Let's say
someone has a thousand dollars..."* — 6M
([themortgagekitchen](https://www.instagram.com/themortgagekitchen/reel/C7ac_3hRMb1/)).
Framework: `It's nuts that most people never question this. Let's say [scenario]...`

---

## 5. Question — *29 hooks*

**Trigger:** opens a loop the viewer's brain auto-tries to answer. A direct
question pulls them out of the scroll to respond.

**Best for:** "did you know," myth-busting, calling out a behavior that should
feel outdated.

**Your angle:** ask the question the creator's ICP (`backbone/icp.md`) is already
asking themselves, or call out the outdated behavior the offer replaces, then
show the fix. Check `knowledge/winning-hooks.md` for your own hits in this shape.
Example (personal-finance creator): "Are you still paying for a subscription you
forgot you had?"

**Proof:** *"Do you realize that every video you watch today is using this
technique to keep you hooked? It's called Pope in the Pool and I'm doing it right
now."* — 1.3M
([viralvideo.club](https://www.instagram.com/viralvideo.club/reel/DFNiMQiubqS/)).
Framework: `Do you realize every [X] uses this to keep you hooked? It's called [Y] and I'm doing it right now.`

---

## 6. Experimentation — *14 hooks*

**Trigger:** a live test with an open outcome. The viewer stays to see if it
works. Built-in stakes + payoff.

**Best for:** "I gave myself X time to..." challenges, zero-to-result runs,
build-in-public sprints.

**Your angle:** a time-bound or constraint-bound test of the creator's own method;
the constraint (time, budget, tools) is what makes it a story, and the frame
justifies a longer watch. Check `knowledge/winning-hooks.md` for your own hits in
this shape. Example (language teacher): "I gave myself 30 days to hold a
conversation in Portuguese; here's what happened."

**Proof:** *"Just a few days ago, we used AI to launch a swimsuit company to see
if we could go from zero to 50,000 in revenue in just 120 days."* — 3.2M
([jimmyslags_](https://www.tiktok.com/@jimmyslags_/video/7241329643565894955)).
Framework: `We used AI to [launch X] to see if we could go from zero to [Y result] in [Z time].`

---

## 7. Fortuneteller — *10 hooks*

**Trigger:** speaks to the viewer's future self — what they'll wish they knew,
how close they are. Identity + aspiration.

**Best for:** slower "if I were starting over" reflection reels, YT long-form
intros, belief-shifting.

**Your angle:** the creator's own origin or turning point (the Backstory section
in `backbone/vision.md`, `voice-dna.md` for how they tell it) aimed at where the
viewer is now. Use sparingly; it's crowded and needs a concrete twist. Example
(photographer): "What I wish I knew before I bought a second lens instead of a
second client."

**Proof:** *"What I wish I knew at 19 instead of 44."* — 19M
([jenlatch](https://www.instagram.com/jenlatch/reel/C3-e4acrDdB/)).
Framework: `What I wish I knew at [X] instead of [Y].`

---

## 8. Comparison — *10 hooks*

**Trigger:** two things side by side with a surprising gap. The viewer has to see
why they differ.

**Best for:** before/after, "same input, wildly different output," this-vs-that.

**Your angle:** the old way vs. The creator's way, side by side, with a gap the
viewer can measure; the numbers come from the proof bank in
`backbone/messaging.md`, and the gap does the work. Example (interior designer):
"Same room, same budget. One got listed for a month, the other sold in a weekend."

**Proof:** *"This is the exact same video, but the second time it was posted it
got 21.4x more views."* — 1M
([viralvideo.club](https://www.instagram.com/viralvideo.club/reel/DBbhGuko0Yn/)).
Framework: `This is the exact same [X]. But the second one got [Y multiplier] more [metric].`

---

## Picking structures for an idea

Most ideas fit 2–4 of these. Match by intent:

- **Showing a system / how-to** → Educational, Secret Reveal
- **Hot take / picking a fight** → Contrarian, Question
- **A result that sounds fake** → Raw Shock, Comparison, Experimentation
- **Belief shift / story** → Fortuneteller, Secret Reveal

Spread the generated hooks across the best-fit structures so the creator has genuinely
different angles to pick from — not five variants of the same line.
